# J01 — CHECKABLE_CUSTOMER_TECH_FACT

**Unit:** one clean customer utterance. **Answers:** YES / NO.

> Does this customer utterance contain a concrete technical parameter, constraint, requirement, or technical question that can be checked, measured, calculated, tested, or answered?

YES requires the utterance itself to state a concrete technical parameter, constraint, requirement, or answerable technical question about the customer's application or proposed equipment. Dimensions, capacities, material or process constraints, and specific compatibility questions qualify. A number is not required if the technical fact is concrete.

NO covers generic interest, acknowledgments, timing or commercial matters, references to missing attachments or measurements, and generic requests for specifications without a specific parameter or constraint. Do not infer content from other messages, attachments, or quoted threads.

Do not judge interest, deal stage, quality of the requirement, or sufficiency of technical data.
